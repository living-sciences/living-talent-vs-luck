2. **MusicLab (Salganik-Dodds-Watts) raw data: NOT STAGED — human gate.** The canonical release (full project: 4 experiments, 27,267 participants, 167 CSVs + documentation) lived at `opr.princeton.edu/archive/cm/`; that host returns 403 to keyless/non-browser access as of 2026-10-22, and the Internet Archive CDX service was temporarily offline during this session, so no verified mirror could be staged. A human with a browser can likely retrieve it in minutes (or request it from the author). Until then, every MusicLab block in the specs runs on its published summary statistics only (Science 311:854-856, 2006: inequality/unpredictability by social-influence condition; "the best songs rarely did poorly, the worst rarely did well, but any other result was possible") and is labelled literature-based.
